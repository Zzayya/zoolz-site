Zoolz Privacy Policy

Zoolz is a personal app used only by its owner. Its Google access is used only with the owner's own Google account.

Zoolz can read and change the owner's own Google Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Gmail drafts and Calendar when he asks it to. That data stays in his Google account and on his own computers.

Zoolz does not sell this data, does not share it with anyone else, and does not collect data from any other person's Google account.
